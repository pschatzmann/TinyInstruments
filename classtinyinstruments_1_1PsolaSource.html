Detailed Description

Pitch shifting which does not change the length of the sound (TD-PSOLA: time domain pitch synchronous overlap and add).

The zone contains pitch marks (the start of each period). A grain covers the two periods around a mark. The grains are placed at the distance of the new period, while the time advances normally, so the pitch changes but the length and the spectral envelope (formants) stay the same.

Because the time is not changed, all grains are close to the current time: the zone is decoded sequentially into a small ring buffer from which the grains are read. The attack (before the first pitch mark) is played unchanged and crossfaded into the grains, so that the onset stays crisp. The output is at the sample rate of the zone: the voice converts it to the output rate.
