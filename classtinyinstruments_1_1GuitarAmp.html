Detailed Description

Amplifier for the direct (DI) signal of an electric guitar or bass: drive with oversampled, slightly asymmetric soft clipping, tone stack (bass, mid, treble), speaker cabinet simulation, stereo chorus and echo.

Midi controllers of the channel:

  • CC16: drive, CC17: tone (treble), CC18: level
  • CC19: preset (0-42 clean, 43-85 crunch, 86-127 lead); the Bass preset is only available in code
  • CC93: chorus depth, CC91: echo level
