Detailed Description

Rotary speaker (Leslie) effect for the TonewheelOrgan, also usable for other instruments (e.g. a guitar or an electric piano).

The signal is split at 800 Hz: the treble goes to a rotating horn, the bass to a rotating drum. Each rotor modulates the pitch (Doppler: modulated delay) and the amplitude; two microphones at 90 degrees give the stereo image. Slow (chorale) and fast (tremolo) speed: the horn accelerates within about 1 s, the heavier drum takes a few seconds, which gives the typical speed change. An optional overdrive (tube preamplifier) is in front.

Midi control changes (on the channels which use the effect):

CC
1 (modulation wheel) fast (>= 64) / slow
26 drive 0 - 127
27 rotor stop (brake, >= 64) / run
