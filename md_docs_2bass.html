Electric Bass

samples/ElectricBass.h contains direct (DI) recordings of a Squier Jazz Bass from C#1 to D#4 (low E string, also tuned down to C#): 14 notes in minor thirds with 2 velocity layers. It needs about 700 KB of flash.

Source: Karoryfer Growlybass (CC0).

Smaller versions: ElectricBassSmall() (~250 KB, samples/ElectricBassSmall.h) and ElectricBassOctave() (~150 KB, samples/ElectricBassOctave.h), see Versions and sizes.

#include "samples/ElectricBass.h"
Instrument bass = BassInstrument(ElectricBass());
GuitarAmp amp(GuitarAmp::Bass); // optional: warm, slightly driven
...
synth.setInstrument(-1, bass);
synth.setEffect(-1, &amp);

See the bass-serial-midi example.

Notes

The notes are mapped at the sounding pitch, like the General MIDI bass programs: the low E string is note 28 (E1, 41 Hz). The original sfz mapping of the library is an octave higher and one zone has a wrong root key: both were corrected by the converter (--transpose -12, --tune-correct).

Playing

  • up to 4 notes at the same time (one per string)
  • palm mute with CC20: notes decay quickly and are darker
  • vibrato with the modulation wheel (CC1), pitch bend
  • the velocity selects the layer (p / f) and the brightness

Bass amplifier

The GuitarAmp::Bass preset keeps the full low end (cabinet low cut 30 Hz, high cut 4 kHz) with a little drive. The guitar presets cut the fundamental of the low E by about 19 dB, so they are not suited for the bass. The cabinet can also be set in code: amp.getSettings().lowCut, highCut, presence.

Flash

Sample data 689 036 bytes + 10 350 bytes pitch marks
bass-serial-midi (I2S + serial midi) 1 152 096 bytes: 87% of the default partition

Pitch shifting

Notes between the recorded notes are created with PSOLA (PitchShift::Psola): the attack and the timbre are the same as in the recording. Set pitchShift = PitchShift::Resample to save CPU (see Instruments).
