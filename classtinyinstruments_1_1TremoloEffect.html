Detailed Description

Tremolo of an electric piano: the built-in "vibrato" of the Wurlitzer (amplitude modulation), or the stereo auto pan of a Rhodes suitcase piano. Also usable for guitars and organs.

Midi control changes (on the channels which use the effect):

CC
1 (modulation wheel) depth
76 rate (1 - 10 Hz)
