Detailed Description

Tonewheel organ (Hammond style): the registration (drawbars, percussion, key click) which is shared by all notes of the instrument. The sound is synthesized: no sample data is needed. Use it with OrganInstrument() and a RotarySpeaker effect.

The 9 drawbars (16', 5 1/3', 8', 4', 2 2/3', 2', 1 3/5', 1 1/3', 1') add sine waves (tonewheels) at the equal tempered pitches of the harmonics; the tonewheels above 5.9 kHz fold back an octave like on the original. Each drawbar step is 3 dB.

Percussion: a decaying 2nd (4') or 3rd (2 2/3') harmonic, single triggered: only when no other key of the channel is held. As on the original the 1' drawbar is silent while the percussion is on, and the normal (loud) percussion reduces the drawbars by 3 dB.

Midi control changes (on the channel of the organ):

CC
12 - 20 drawbars 16' ... 1' (0 - 127 -> 0 - 8)
21 percussion on (>= 64) / off
22 percussion harmonic: 3rd (>= 64) / 2nd
23 percussion decay: fast (>= 64) / slow
24 percussion volume: soft (>= 64) / normal
25 key click amount

The settings can be changed while notes are playing.
